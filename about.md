## Who we are

I'm {{ site.founder_name }}, a student in Chandigarh. I started Aagaaz in 2026.

Over several months I spoke to lawyers, counsellors, a university student welfare head, a child rights official and a school harassment committee head. I kept hearing the same thing: the help exists, but people don't know about it. Free medical care. A free lawyer. The right to give a statement at home instead of at a police station. Most people find out too late, or never.

Aagaaz means "a beginning". The aim is simple: from awareness to action.

## What we do

- **This website,** with short, plain-language pages on what to do and what the law says
- **A navigation toolkit** for Chandigarh, Panchkula and Mohali, which we are finishing now
- **Workshops** in schools and colleges, run with the school's counsellor in the room

## What we are not

Aagaaz is a student-led project. It is not a registered charity, a helpline, a counselling service or a law firm. We cannot respond to emergencies and we cannot give advice on your case.
{: .note}

## How we check what we publish

- Everything here is based on the law itself and on interviews with people who work with survivors
- Every page shows the date it was last updated
- Each law page lists its sources at the bottom, so anyone can check it
- If you find a mistake, please tell us. We will fix it

## Your privacy on this site

- There are no logins and no forms. We never ask for your name or your story
- There are no cookies, no adverts and no tracking or analytics
- Like every website, the company that hosts this site receives your device's IP address when a page loads. We never see it
- Your browser may still keep a record of your visit. [Staying safe on this site](/safety/) explains what to do about that
