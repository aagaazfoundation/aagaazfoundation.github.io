## If you need to leave quickly

- **Close the tab,** or switch to another app
- Closing the tab does not clear your history. The sections below explain how
- If you only switch apps, this page may still be open when someone picks up your phone

## Keep your visit out of your history

**Use a private window.** Pages you open in it are not saved to your history.

- **Chrome:** tap the three dots, then "New Incognito tab"
- **Safari on iPhone:** tap the tabs button, then "Private"
- **Samsung Internet:** tap the tabs button, then "Turn on Secret mode"

**Or delete this site from your history afterwards.** In most browsers: open History from the menu, find the pages from this site, and remove them.

## If someone checks your phone

- A call to 112, 181, 1098 or 1930 shows in your call log. You can delete a single call from the log
- If you are worried about someone seeing this site on your phone, use a device they don't have access to, like a friend's phone or a school or library computer
- Screenshots save to your photo gallery. Delete them, or write the numbers down somewhere safe instead
