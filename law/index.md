## Two things to know first

**The law is the same in all three cities.** Chandigarh is a Union Territory, Panchkula is in Haryana and Mohali is in Punjab. The offices and phone numbers are run by three different governments, but your rights do not change when you cross a city border. You can go to whichever police station or hospital is nearest.

**The section numbers changed in 2024.** On 1 July 2024 the Indian Penal Code was replaced by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), and the Code of Criminal Procedure by the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS). Many websites still quote the old numbers. This site uses the new ones.

## This is information, not legal advice

These pages explain the law in general. They cannot tell you what will happen in your case. A lawyer can, and it does not have to cost anything: every woman and every child is entitled to a free lawyer, whatever their income. Call the legal aid helpline on {% include call.html line="legal-aid" %}.
