## Good to know

- **Any police station must take your complaint,** wherever it happened. You don't have to find "the right one". [More on reporting](/law/adults/)
- **Every hospital, private ones too, must treat you free of cost** after rape, or after sexual assault of anyone under 18. For the medical examination that is used as evidence, go to a government hospital. [What to do first](/first-steps/)
- **Call before you go if you can.** Opening hours change. Each place shows the date we last checked it
- **If you are under 18,** you can also call the Child Helpline on {% include call.html line="child" %} and they will tell you where to go

This list is not complete. If a place is not here, it only means we have not checked it yet. If something on this page is wrong or out of date, please tell us: the address is on the [About](/about/) page.
