# Report an unsafe place: setting up the forms

The page is at `/unsafe-places/` (Hindi: `/hi/unsafe-places/`). It uses **two Google Forms**, both in English and Hindi:

1. **The report form.** No sign-in, no name, no email. Anyone can use it.
2. **A separate photo and video form**, for anyone who wants to send a photo or video too. Google makes everyone sign in to a form that has a photo upload, so it is kept separate. That way the main form stays open to people who don't want to sign in.

Copy each block below into Google Forms exactly as written. The English comes first, then the Hindi.

---

## Form 1: the report form

### Title

```
Report an unsafe place / असुरक्षित जगह के बारे में बताएँ
```

### Description

```
Tell us about places that feel unsafe, like a broken streetlight or a dark lane. We will take this to the municipality.
This form is not for reporting something that happened to you. In danger? Call 112. Under 18? Call 1098.

ऐसी जगहों के बारे में बताएँ जो असुरक्षित लगती हैं, जैसे ख़राब स्ट्रीटलाइट या अँधेरी गली। हम यह जानकारी नगर निगम तक पहुँचाएँगे।
यह फ़ॉर्म आपके साथ हुई किसी घटना की शिकायत के लिए नहीं है। अगर आप ख़तरे में हैं, तो 112 पर कॉल करें। अगर आपकी उम्र 18 साल से कम है, तो 1098 पर कॉल करें।
```

### Questions

**1.** `District / ज़िला` (Multiple choice, required)
- `Chandigarh / चंडीगढ़`
- `Panchkula / पंचकूला`
- `Mohali / मोहाली`

**2.** `Area or sector / इलाक़ा या सेक्टर` (Short answer, required)
Description: `For example: Sector 22 / जैसे: सेक्टर 22`

**3.** `Where exactly is it? / यह जगह ठीक-ठीक कहाँ है?` (Paragraph, required)
Description: `Mention a landmark nearby. For example: the lane behind the market / आसपास की कोई पहचान वाली जगह (लैंडमार्क) लिखें। जैसे: मार्केट के पीछे वाली गली`

**4.** `What is the problem? / समस्या क्या है?` (Checkboxes, required)
- `Streetlight not working / स्ट्रीटलाइट काम नहीं करती`
- `No lights at all / वहाँ कोई लाइट नहीं है`
- `Bushes or walls block the view / झाड़ियों या दीवारों की वजह से कुछ दिखाई नहीं देता`
- `Empty, no one around / सुनसान जगह, आसपास कोई नहीं होता`
- `Underpass, park or parking area / अंडरपास, पार्क या पार्किंग`
- `Bus stop / बस स्टॉप`
- Turn on **Add "Other"**. Google shows the word "Other" itself, so there is nothing to type for it.

**5.** `When does it feel unsafe? / यह जगह कब असुरक्षित लगती है?` (Checkboxes, optional)
- `After dark / अँधेरा होने के बाद`
- `Early morning / सुबह-सुबह`
- `All the time / हर समय`

**6.** `Describe the place in as much detail as you can / इस जगह के बारे में जितना हो सके, विस्तार से लिखें` (Paragraph, optional)
Description:
```
For example: how long the dark stretch is, what is around it, and since when it has been like this. Only write about the place. Don't write about something that happened to a person, and don't name anyone.
जैसे: अँधेरा रास्ता कितना लंबा है, आसपास क्या है, और यह कब से ऐसा है। सिर्फ़ जगह के बारे में लिखें। किसी इंसान के साथ हुई घटना के बारे में न लिखें, और किसी का नाम न लें।
```

### Settings (important)

Tap **Settings** at the top of the form:

- **Responses → Collect email addresses:** Do not collect
- **Responses → Limit to 1 response:** off (turning it on makes people sign in)
- **Don't add a File upload question to this form.** It would make everyone sign in.
- **Presentation → Confirmation message** (make Form 2 first, so you can paste its link here):

```
Thank you. We read every report. Have a photo or video of this place? Send it here: PASTE THE PHOTO FORM LINK
धन्यवाद। हम हर रिपोर्ट पढ़ते हैं। क्या आपके पास इस जगह की फ़ोटो या वीडियो है? यहाँ भेजें: PASTE THE PHOTO FORM LINK
Need help? Call 112, 1098 or 181. / मदद चाहिए तो 112, 1098 या 181 पर कॉल करें।
```

## Form 2: the photo form

### Title

```
Send a photo or video of an unsafe place / असुरक्षित जगह की फ़ोटो या वीडियो भेजें
```

### Description

```
You need to sign in to Google to upload a photo or video, so we will see the name on your Google account. We use them only for the report and never share your name.
Take it in daylight. Make sure no people or vehicle number plates are in it. Don't put yourself at risk to take it.

फ़ोटो या वीडियो अपलोड करने के लिए Google में साइन इन करना होगा, इसलिए हमें आपके Google अकाउंट का नाम दिखेगा। इनका इस्तेमाल सिर्फ़ रिपोर्ट के लिए होगा, और आपका नाम कभी किसी को नहीं बताया जाएगा।
फ़ोटो या वीडियो दिन की रोशनी में लें। ध्यान रखें कि उसमें कोई इंसान या गाड़ी का नंबर न दिखे। इसके लिए ख़ुद को ख़तरे में न डालें।
```

### Questions

**1.** `Area or sector / इलाक़ा या सेक्टर` (Short answer, required)

**2.** `Where exactly is it? / यह जगह ठीक-ठीक कहाँ है?` (Short answer, required)

**3.** `Photo or video / फ़ोटो या वीडियो` (File upload, required)
- Allow only specific file types: **Image** and **Video**
- Maximum number of files: **3**
- Maximum file size: **100 MB** (videos are big; 10 MB would block most of them)

### Settings

- **Responses → Collect email addresses:** Do not collect
- **Confirmation message:**

```
Thank you. / धन्यवाद।
```

## Put the links on the site

1. In each form, tap **Send**, then the link icon, and copy the link.
2. In GitHub, open `_config.yml` and tap the pencil. Paste the report form's link between the quotes on `form_url: ""`, and the photo form's link on `photo_form_url: ""`. Commit.

Each button appears on the English and Hindi pages about a minute after its link is added. You can add the report form first and the photo form later.

## Reading the answers

- The answers are in each form's **Responses** tab. The photos and videos go to a folder in the foundation's Google Drive.
- Before anything goes into the report, delete any answer that describes something that happened to a person or names someone.
- Delete any photo or video that shows a face or a number plate, or blur it before you use it.
- If an answer suggests a child is being hurt, ring 1098, tell them what it says, and tell your mentor. Don't try to reply to it yourself.
