# Legal review checklist: Aagaaz Foundation website

For the reviewing advocate. 11 November 2026, updated 16 November 2026.

The website's phase 1 pages make the claims below. Each is written for a general reader, including children, so the wording is deliberately simple. Please mark anything that is wrong, out of date, or simplified to the point of being misleading. Section numbers appear on the site only inside a fold-out "Where this comes from" table at the bottom of each law page.

**Status of this content:** drafted from the Aagaaz toolkit drafts and the bare Acts, and checked against the Acts online on 16 November 2026. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer. The site is not listed on search engines until this review is done.

## A. Points I am least sure of. Please check these first

On 16 November 2026 each point below was checked against the text of the Acts and other sources online. The right-hand column says what that check found. It is not a substitute for your review.

| # | Page | Claim on the site now | Online check, 16 Nov 2026 |

"Unless she chooses" added |

## B. Every other claim, page by page

### What to do first
- After rape, or sexual assault of anyone under 18, every hospital must treat free of cost (BNSS s. 397: BNS ss. 64 to 68, 70, 71, 124(1); POCSO ss. 4, 6, 8, 10)
- Medicine to prevent HIV must be started within 72 hours; emergency contraception within 72 hours, some types up to 120 hours (Ministry of Health guidelines)
- Under 18: an adult who is told must report to the police (POCSO s. 19)
- Under 18: a doctor can examine and treat before any police report (POCSO s. 27); the doctor will inform the police
- Under 18: statement at home or a chosen place, woman officer not in uniform, trusted person present (POCSO ss. 24, 26)
- 18 or older: medical care, police report and trial are separate decisions
- 18 or older: the hospital must inform the police, but the survivor chooses whether to make a complaint (BNSS s. 397)
- 18 or older: FIR at any police station (BNSS s. 173)

### Know the law: Adults
- BNS rape and sexual harassment offences protect women only; men and transgender adults are pointed to other offences, the IT Act and the Transgender Persons Act 2019
- Offences table: BNS ss. 63 and 64 (rape), 74, 75, 76, 77, 78, 79, each with a one-line plain description
- "Not fighting back is not consent" (BNS s. 63, Explanation 2)
- Zero FIR; free copy; complaint recorded by a woman officer (BNSS s. 173)
- No time limit for reporting rape; some lesser offences have limits (BNSS s. 514)
- Magistrate records the statement, by a woman Magistrate where practicable (BNSS s. 183(6)(a)). The earlier wording "in private, without the accused present" was removed because s. 183(6) does not say it

### Know the law: Under 18
- POCSO covers every child, of any gender
- Mandatory reporting; failure is an offence (ss. 19, 21)
- Police inform the Child Welfare Committee (s. 19(6))
- Statement: home or chosen place, woman officer of sub-inspector rank or above, not in uniform, not kept overnight (s. 24)
- Magistrate may record the statement, trusted person present (ss. 25, 26)
- Girl examined by a woman doctor, trusted person present, possible before an FIR, free (s. 27)
- Special Court; no sight of the accused; closed hearing; breaks; not called repeatedly (ss. 28, 33, 36, 37)
- Evidence within 30 days and trial within a year, "in practice it often takes longer" (s. 35)
- Identity protected (s. 23); support person (s. 39, POCSO Rules 2020); lawyer (s. 40); compensation, some paid early (s. 33(8), Rules)

### Know the law: Workplace and campus
- POSH Act 2013 is a route separate from the police; both can be used; employer must assist with a police complaint (s. 19(g))
- Protects any woman, employed or not (s. 2(a)); wide meaning of workplace (s. 2(o))
- What counts (ss. 2(n), 3)
- Internal Committee for 10 or more employees, led by a senior woman, half women, one external member (s. 4)
- Local Committee for under 10 employees, complaints against the employer, and domestic workers (ss. 5, 6)
- Written complaint within 3 months, extendable by 3 months; committee helps with writing (s. 9)
- Inquiry within 90 days (s. 11(4)); transfer or leave during inquiry (s. 12); confidentiality (s. 16)
- SHe-Box portal: shebox.wcd.gov.in
- Colleges must have an ICC under the UGC 2015 Regulations; PUCASH at Panjab University

### Know the law: Rights for everyone
- Free, immediate treatment at every hospital, government or private; refusing is an offence (BNSS s. 397, BNS s. 200)
- Consent to each part of the examination; two-finger test banned (State of Jharkhand v Shailendra Kumar Rai, 2022)
- Free legal aid for every woman and child regardless of income (Legal Services Authorities Act s. 12); helpline 15100
- Compensation separate from conviction, part payable early, applied for through the Legal Services Authority (BNSS s. 396, NALSA 2018 scheme)
- Identity not to be published after rape or any sexual offence against a child (BNS s. 72 covers ss. 64 to 71; POCSO s. 23); closed hearings (BNSS s. 366(2), POCSO s. 37)

### Online abuse
- Don't pay, don't delete, tell someone, report to the platform, report at cybercrime.gov.in or 1930
- Take It Down for images of under-18s; StopNCII.org for adults
- Fake or AI-made sexual images are handled the same way
- Keeping or sharing a sexual image of anyone under 18 is an offence (POCSO s. 15, IT Act s. 67B)
- Offences referred to in the sources table: IT Act ss. 66E, 67, 67A, 67B; BNS ss. 77, 78, 308, 351

### Find help near you (built 17 Nov 2026, switched off until places are verified)
- "Any police station must take your complaint, wherever it happened" (BNSS s. 173(1))
- "Every hospital, private ones too, must treat you free of cost after rape, or after sexual assault of anyone under 18" (BNSS s. 397)
- "For the medical examination that is used as evidence, go to a government hospital" (BNSS s. 184(1))
- One Stop Centres described as "for women and girls: medical, police, legal and counselling help in one place, and a short stay"
- Legal aid offices described as "free lawyers for every woman and every child; they also help with compensation"

### Helping someone else
- An adult who knows a child has been sexually abused must report; not reporting is an offence (POCSO ss. 19, 21)
- "Don't investigate first. You don't need proof."
- For an adult survivor, what happens next is their decision

## D. The Hindi version

Every page also exists in Hindi (under `aagaazfoundation.org/hi/`). It is a translation of the English pages, written in everyday spoken Hindi. It was published on 15 November 2026 and fully revised the same day for natural phrasing.

It needs one read by someone fluent in Hindi, ideally with legal Hindi. Please check in particular:

- **English in brackets on first use:** यौन उत्पीड़न (sexual harassment), यौन शोषण (sexual abuse), यौन हमला (sexual assault), बलात्कार (rape), सहमति (consent), मुआवज़ा (compensation), बयान (statement)
- **Offence names in the Adults table** follow the official Hindi text of the BNS where I could (for example स्त्री की लज्जा भंग करने के इरादे से हमला, दृश्यरतिकता). The left-hand column describes each offence in plain words. Are both right?
- **Other terms:** FIR (प्रथम सूचना रिपोर्ट); पॉक्सो (POCSO), लैंगिक अपराधों से बालकों का संरक्षण अधिनियम 2012; ज़िला विधिक सेवा प्राधिकरण (DLSA); बाल कल्याण समिति; आंतरिक समिति and स्थानीय समिति; विशेष अदालत (स्पेशल कोर्ट); सहायक व्यक्ति (सपोर्ट पर्सन)
- **"Adult" is written as बड़े (18 साल या उससे ज़्यादा उम्र के व्यक्ति)**, not वयस्क, so children understand it
- **"You will not get into trouble" is written as "आप किसी मुसीबत में नहीं पड़ेंगे"** (see also point A7)
- The Workplace page addresses the reader in the feminine (आप कर सकती हैं), because the POSH Act protects women. Other pages use the usual generic forms
- The "What to say" list on the Helping page uses तुम, because that is how a friend would speak
- Anything that still reads as stiff or unclear to a 13-year-old or a parent
- The new "Find help near you" page (अपने पास मदद ढूँढें), added 17 November 2026 and not yet public: its buttons, messages and the "ये बातें जान लें" list

Any change to the English wording after this review must be made in the Hindi page too.
